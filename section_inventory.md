### Section-specific design notes
- **Hero:** Large typographic treatment on the tagline. "OPENING SOON · KOKAPET, HYDERABAD" as a small eyebrow label above. Hero image = facility exterior render or construction-site progress photo [PENDING: check client_assets/images/]. If no image: full navy background with teal geometric accent.
- **Our Story:** Text-led, single column, generous white space. Pull quote treatment: *"UniCare exists to end that compromise."* No stock imagery — this section is about words and emotional truth.
- **Services Preview:** Icon + title + 1-sentence description per specialty. Grid: 3×2 on desktop, 2×3 on mobile. Teal icons on white cards, navy label. Bottom note: "Plus 24/7 emergency, ICU, in-house pharmacy, and ambulance services." in smaller type.
- **Founded by Doctors:** Could use a navy full-bleed section with white text — creates drama and premium feel. Abstract visual (e.g. blurred silhouette, architectural photo of a corridor, or a purposeful white-space layout). No named doctors, no face photos yet.
- **Waitlist Form:** Navy background with white form card OR white section with navy form card — the form must visually pop. "First 500 families" counter/progress bar if technically feasible. CTA button in teal.
- **Location:** Light grey background. Map on right, proximity callouts on left (with distance pill labels in teal). Clean, not cluttered.
- **Follow the Journey:** White background. Social icon + handle + 1-line description. Optional: live Instagram feed widget (shows actual construction/behind-the-scenes posts if available).
- **Footer:** Navy background, white text. Teal accent on email links. Keep minimal — logo, tagline, two emails, address, social icons, privacy policy link.

### CTA hierarchy (pre-launch specific)
- Primary CTA: "NOTIFY ME WHEN WE OPEN →" / "RESERVE MY SPOT →" — teal or navy filled button
- Secondary CTA: Social follow links — outlined or ghost button style
- No "Book Appointment" CTA — hospital is not open
- No phone number CTA — optional if client wants to field pre-launch enquiries, but not required
