# Unicare Hospitals — Pre-Launch Page
## Section Design Ideas

---

## Section 1 — HEADER

### Desktop

**Option A — Floating Glass Bar**
Centered logo · "OPENING SOON" pill badge · social icons right-aligned. On scroll: frosted-glass blur appears behind the bar — no layout shift, no hard colour change.

**Option B — Full-Width Ruled Header**
Logo left · "OPENING SOON · KOKAPET" centered as plain text · social icons right. A single 1px bottom border separates it from the page. On scroll: background fills in solidly.

**Option C — Hairline Minimal**
Logo only, left-aligned, very small. "OPENING SOON" appears as a badge directly beneath the logo. Social icons fade in from the top-right only after the user begins scrolling — maximum hero visibility before that.

### Mobile

**Centered Stack:** Logo centered · "OPENING SOON" badge beneath · social icons in a row below. Clean, symmetrical, reads like a formal announcement.

**Split Row:** Logo left · social icons right (3 icons max). "OPENING SOON" badge is hidden from the header entirely and shown inside the hero instead — conserves space.

---

## Section 2 — HERO

### Desktop

**Option A — Dark Cinematic Split**
Left half: large stacked headline. "OPENING SOON · KOKAPET, HYDERABAD" as a small eyebrow label above. Primary CTA button sits directly beneath the headline.
Right half: facility render or construction photo, edge-to-edge, no padding.

**Option B — Full Bleed Image with Overlay**
Full-viewport background image. Gradient overlay from left or bottom. Headline and CTA anchored bottom-left in an editorial grid. "OPENING SOON" badge floats top-left. Works powerfully if the facility render is strong.

**Option C — Typography-Only Hero (No Image)**
Full dark background, no photo. A very large Georgia headline fills the top 60% of the screen — the type itself becomes the visual. A subtle teal geometric accent (diagonal line or corner bracket) adds texture. Elements animate in sequentially: eyebrow → headline → CTA.

**Option D — Countdown Hero**
Large countdown timer (Days · Hours · Minutes · Seconds) in display numerals dominates the upper half. Tagline and CTA sit below. Only use this if a hard launch date is confirmed.

### Mobile

**Stacked Layout:** Eyebrow label → headline (2 lines) → subline → full-width CTA button. Image appears below the fold or as a heavily overlaid background.

**Typography-Only Mobile:** Dark background, large headline, no image. Fastest to load, cleanest on small screens.

**Image-Background Mobile:** Facility photo full height, dark overlay, white text, CTA pinned just above the bottom edge of the screen.

## Section 3 — OUR STORY

### Desktop

**Option A — Single Column Editorial**
Narrow centered column (max ~680px). Pull quote breaks out of the column in large Georgia italic with a left vertical rule accent. No images. White background. Pure typographic authority — like a well-written newspaper feature.

**Option B — Two Column with Visual Anchor**
Text column on the left. Right column holds a single strong visual — an architectural interior detail, a blurred corridor, or a full dark abstract panel. Keeps the copy from feeling isolated on wide screens.

**Option C — Dark Full-Bleed Chapter Break**
The entire section becomes a dark background with white text. Large Georgia italic pull quote centered at the top creates a dramatic pause between the hero and the services section. The change in background signals: *this part of the story matters.*

**Option D — Scrolling Reveal**
Paragraphs or key sentences animate in line by line as the user scrolls. Each reveal is a fade + upward slide. Adds ~8–10 seconds of intentional reading time. Works for brands that want storytelling to feel cinematic.

### Mobile

**Single column, full width.** Pull quote gets a larger type treatment, centered. The vertical rule accent becomes a short horizontal rule above the quote.

**Pull quote as a dark card.** The quote sits inside a full-bleed dark card that breaks the page margin. Body text on white above and below it. Creates a strong visual moment mid-scroll.

## Section 4 — SERVICES PREVIEW

### Desktop

**Option A — 3×2 Icon Card Grid**
Six equal cards in a 3-column grid. Each card: icon top · specialty name · one-sentence description. Hover state: card lifts slightly with a stronger shadow + accent border appears. Feels structured and scannable. A small supporting line below the grid covers emergency, ICU, and ambulance.

**Option B — Horizontal Scroll Ribbon**
All six cards in a single wide row with enough width to hint at overflow. Desktop shows 4.5 cards, prompting a natural scroll. Each card is wider with more breathing room. Works well if a hover/expand interaction is added later.

**Option C — Alternating Row Backgrounds**
Three cards per row. Row 1 sits on white. Row 2 sits on a very pale tint of the accent colour. The alternation creates visual rhythm without a grid feeling monotonous.

**Option D — Dark Section with Glowing Cards**
Dark section background. Each card is a slightly lighter dark panel with an icon and white text. On hover: a soft accent-coloured glow appears at the card border. Creates a premium, high-contrast visual — closer to a tech company aesthetic.

### Mobile

**2×3 Grid:** Two columns, three rows. Compact cards with icon and specialty name. Description truncated or hidden.

**Horizontal Swipe Row:** Single row, user swipes to reveal all six. A swipe-hint arrow or partial card edge signals interactivity.

**Stacked List:** Single column. Icon left, specialty name + one line of text right. No card border — just spacing and a subtle divider line. Most compact option.

## Section 5 — FOUNDED BY DOCTORS

### Desktop

**Option A — Dark Full-Bleed Narrative**
The entire section is a dark background. White body copy in a centered column. A short, powerful Georgia headline at the top. No images, no names, no photos — the restraint itself communicates confidence.

**Option B — Split: Prose Left, Abstract Visual Right**
Left: white background text column with the founding narrative. Right: a dark panel — could hold an anonymised silhouette, a blurred architectural interior, or a large typographic poster element (e.g., "Est. 2026" treated as art).

**Option C — Large Pull Quote Layout**
The section is built almost entirely around one powerful quote in very large Georgia italic — centered, wide, on white. The founder attribution sits below in small caps. A short supporting paragraph follows in standard body type. The quote functions as both the message and the visual.

**Option D — Values Strip + Narrative**
A horizontal row of 3–4 founder values with icons (e.g., Doctor-Led · Patient-First · Community-Focused). Below it, the full founding story in prose. Useful when the philosophy needs to be communicated explicitly, not just evoked.

### Mobile

**Dark full-bleed:** Pull quote in large italic centered. Body text below. A subtle secondary CTA at the bottom scrolls to the waitlist form.

**White section with dark card insert:** Body text on white above. The pull quote drops into a full-bleed dark card (negative-margin, edge-to-edge). Body text continues on white below.

## Section 6 — WAITLIST / LEAD CAPTURE FORM

### Desktop

**Option A — White Card on Dark Background (Recommended)**
Dark section background. A centered white card (rounded corners, generous internal padding) holds the form. Above or beside the card: "Join the first 500 families" with a visual progress counter showing spots claimed. Benefits checklist anchored left of the card. CTA button: full width inside the card.

**Option B — Split: Story Left, Form Right**
Two columns. Left column: section headline + benefits list + social proof number ("312 families already registered"). Right column: the white form card, vertically centered. Lets you sell the value proposition while the form is simultaneously visible.

**Option C — Centered on White, No Card**
White section. Form fields floating on white with generous spacing — no card border. Teal accent on the section heading. CTA button is the only strong colour in the section. Clean, minimal, faster to scan.

**Option D — Dark Background with Geometric Pattern**
Dark section background with a very low-opacity geometric texture (thin grid lines or diagonal hatching). White form card centered. The texture adds depth without distracting from the form.

### Progress Counter Variants
- **Text only:** "Join 312 other families" — minimal, always works
- **Progress bar:** "312 of 500 spots claimed" + a filled bar at 62%
- **Animated number:** Counter increments visually when section scrolls into view
- **Avatar cluster:** Small overlapping avatar circles + "and 312 others have joined"

### Mobile

**Full-width form on white.** Each field full-width. Benefits shown as a horizontal scroll strip of pill tags above the form ("✓ Priority booking · ✓ Launch date first"). CTA button full width, sticky-pinned at the bottom of the viewport while the form is visible.

**Dark background, white form card edge-to-edge.** Progress bar above the card. CTA full width inside the card.

## Section 7 — LOCATION

### Desktop

**Option A — Callouts Left, Map Right**
Two-column layout. Left: section heading + three proximity callouts displayed as pill chips with a location pin icon and a distance label. Full address below with a "Get Directions →" link. Right: Google Maps embed with a branded pin, no other map controls visible.

**Option B — Full-Width Map with Overlay Card**
Google Maps embed spans the full section width (tall). A floating card overlays the bottom-left or top-left of the map — contains the address + proximity chips. The map stays immersive behind the card.

**Option C — Illustrated Map (No Google Maps)**
A custom SVG illustration of the local area. Major landmarks (Financial District, Narsingi, Gachibowli) are drawn as simple icons. The hospital is marked at the center with the brand pin. Lines radiate outward to each landmark with a distance label. Completely branded — no third-party map tiles.

**Option D — Distance Ring Diagram**
A circular infographic: the hospital at the centre, concentric rings at 5 / 10 / 15 minute intervals, neighbourhood names positioned on the rings. Clean, abstract, immediately communicates catchment area in a single glance.

### Mobile

**Chips above map:** Proximity pill chips in a horizontal scroll row at the top. Map full width below (shorter height, ~240px). Address + directions link below the map.

**Chips stacked below map:** Map full width at top. Below: each proximity callout as a full-width row with icon + name + time. Address at the very bottom.

## Section 8 — FOLLOW THE JOURNEY

### Desktop

**Option A — Social Card Row**
Three equal cards side by side: Instagram · YouTube · LinkedIn. Each card: platform icon + handle + one-line description + "Follow →" outlined button. White section background. Simple and reliable.

**Option B — Live Instagram Feed**
Section heading + two lines of copy at the top. Below: a grid of live Instagram post thumbnails (6–9 posts) pulled from the @unicarehospital feed showing real construction progress. A "Follow on Instagram →" button below the grid. Dynamic and authentic — only works if the account is actively posting.

**Option C — Featured Video**
A large video thumbnail with a play button overlay dominates the section. Below or beside: short copy + social follow links. Works if a construction timelapse, founder video, or facility walkthrough exists.

**Option D — Milestone Timeline Strip**
A horizontal timeline: key construction milestones marked as points with short labels and dates (Foundation · Structure · Fit-out · Opening). Social follow links sit beneath. Creates narrative investment — visitors feel part of the story as it unfolds.

### Mobile

**Stacked social cards:** Each card full width, icon left, handle + button right. Clean and compact.

**Instagram grid (2 columns):** 4–6 posts in a 2-column thumbnail grid. Tapping opens the Instagram app. Follow button below the grid.

## Section 9 — FOOTER

### Desktop

**Option A — Minimal Two-Row Footer**
Dark background. Full width, two rows:
Row 1: Logo left · short tagline centered · social icons right.
Row 2 (subdued, smaller type): contact emails · address · Privacy Policy · copyright.

**Option B — Footer with Top Accent Border**
Identical to Option A but with a 2px accent-coloured top border on the footer. Creates a clean visual separator from the section above — particularly useful when the section above is also dark.

**Option C — Two-Column Footer**
Left column: Logo + tagline + social icons.
Right column: contact emails, address, privacy link.
Best when the contact details are long enough to need their own column.

### Mobile

**Centered stack:** Logo + tagline centered · social icon row centered · contact details centered in smaller type · privacy + copyright at the very bottom.

**Left-aligned stack:** Everything left-aligned. More editorial, less formal. Each element sits on its own line with comfortable spacing.

## Floating / Global Elements

### WhatsApp Button
Circular floating button, bottom-right. Appears after the user scrolls 40% of the page — doesn't compete with the hero. Label: "Chat with us" appears on hover (desktop) or always visible (mobile).

### Sticky Mobile CTA Bar
On mobile only: once the hero CTA scrolls off screen, a slim bar appears pinned at the bottom of the viewport. Short copy + "Reserve your spot →" link. Tapping smoothly scrolls to the waitlist form. Ensures the primary conversion action is always one tap away.

### Cookie / Consent Banner
Slim bar at the very bottom of the viewport on first visit. Minimal copy: "We use cookies to improve your experience." + Accept button. Dismisses on click.
