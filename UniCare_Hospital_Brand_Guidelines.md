# UniCare Hospital — Brand Guidelines

> **Tagline:** Expert care, close to home.

---

## 1. Brand Story

At UniCare Hospital, the best healthcare is not just defined by expertise — but by how close it feels to you. In moments of uncertainty, patients seek reassurance, accessibility, and trust.

UniCare was built to deliver world-class medical expertise while remaining deeply connected to the communities it serves.

**We bring together:**
- Experienced specialists
- Advanced medical technology
- Patient-first care systems

**Core Promise:** Great healthcare should never feel distant or complicated. It should feel accessible, reliable, and close — just like home.

## 2. Logo Construction

| Element | Description |
|---|---|
| **UC Monogram** | Interconnected "U" & "C" — represents unity, continuity, and patient journey |
| **Flowing Form** | Inspired by the infinity loop — symbolizes lifelong care and ongoing support |
| **Colour Fusion** | Navy = Trust & expertise · Teal = Healing & compassion |
| **Hospital Badge** | Structured capsule form — institutional authority with human touch |

### Logo Usage Rules

**✅ Correct**
- Maintain clear space around the logo
- Use on white or navy backgrounds only
- Keep proportions intact
- Use the official colour palette

**❌ Incorrect**
- Stretch or distort the logo
- Change colours randomly
- Add shadows or effects
- Place on cluttered backgrounds

## 5. Brand Voice

> *"We speak like a trusted doctor — not a corporation."*

| Voice Pillar | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Trusted** | Warm, reassuring, personal | *"You came to the right place. We'll take good care of you."* |
| **Healing** | Speaks to clinical expertise | *"Our orthopaedic team has performed 500+ joint replacements."* |
| **Honest** | Data-driven, educational | *"8 hours of sitting raises back pain risk by 40%. Here's what to do."* |
| **Human** | Accessible, action-oriented | *"UniCare Kokapet. Open now. Book in 2 minutes."* |

**Patient-facing tone:** Warm · Reassuring · Personal · Speaks to the emotion behind the visit.

## 6. Target Audience

### Primary — Young IT Families (Age 25–38)
- Dual-income IT couples · ₹1.5L–6L household income
- Located in Kokapet, Narsingi premium gated communities
- Expecting or with children under 5
- **Decision-maker:** The mother
- **Entry Services:** Maternity · Paediatrics · Women's Health

### Secondary — IT Working Professionals (Age 28–50)
- Mid-to-senior IT professionals · ₹80K–3L household income
- Located in Financial District, Gachibowli, Manikonda
- Lifestyle conditions: back pain, diabetes
- **Decision-maker:** The individual
- **Entry Services:** Orthopaedics · General Medicine · Diagnostics

### Tertiary — Corporate HR Managers (Age 30–55)
- HR managers at IT companies within 7–10 km
- Managing 100–1,000 employees
- Annual contract buyers
- **Entry Services:** Corporate health · Cashless OPD · On-site camps
